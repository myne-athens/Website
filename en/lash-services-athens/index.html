Lash questions

What is a lash lift?

A treatment that lifts and curls your natural lashes. Nothing is added; your own lashes are brought forward.

How much is a lash lift at MYNE?

€35 for a lash lift and €40 with a tint. A lash tint on its own is €10.

How long does the appointment take?

About 55 minutes for a lash lift and 50 minutes with a tint. Pending: confirm both durations

Can I combine brows and lashes?
