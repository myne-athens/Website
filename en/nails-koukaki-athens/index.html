Beautiful nails begin with the right care.

At MYNE in Koukaki, every manicure and pedicure starts with your own nails. We look at how they are today, what they need and how you live, then choose the treatment together.

Care comes before colour

Colour is the last step. Before it comes the nail itself: its shape, its strength, the skin around it. That is why we don't recommend the same thing to everyone. A simple manicure suits one person, reinforced semi-permanent another, a break from product a third.

We tell you what we see and what we suggest. You decide.

What we can do for your nails

Manicure

Shaping, cuticle care and attention to the natural nail. Left bare, finished with regular polish, or with semi-permanent polish, depending on how long you want it to last.

Strength for natural nails

If your nails break or peel easily, reinforced semi-permanent polish and natural nail gel reinforcement add resilience without adding length.

Gel and acrygel

A new set for length and shape the natural nail cannot give, and maintenance as it grows out.

Pedicure

Care for feet and nails, finished with regular or semi-permanent polish. Pending: what the therapeutic and spa pedicures include

Removal

Proper removal protects the nail as much as proper application. We take the time it needs.

For men

Manicures and pedicures for men, with the same care.

Nail care questions

Where can I get a manicure in Koukaki, Athens?

At MYNE Nails & Brows, Kallirrois 74. A simple manicure without polish costs €15 and takes 30 minutes; a semi-permanent manicure costs €20 and takes 45 minutes. Book online or call +30 210 923 6859.

Where can I find personalised nail care in Koukaki?

At MYNE, the treatment is chosen after we look at the condition of your natural nails. If they need reinforcement or a rest, we'll say so.

How much is a semi-permanent manicure?

€20, or €25 for reinforced semi-permanent. Removing old semi-permanent polish costs €5.

What is the difference between regular and reinforced semi-permanent?

The reinforced version adds a strengthening layer under the colour, for nails that need more resilience. It takes about 60 minutes instead of 45.

Do you offer pedicures?

Yes. A simple pedicure is €25, semi-permanent €28 and therapeutic €28. A men's pedicure is €18.

Do you do nail art and French tips?

Yes, at an additional cost on top of the service you choose.
