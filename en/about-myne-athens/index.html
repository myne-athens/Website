What we believe

MYNE is not simply another nail, brow and lash salon. It is a personalised beauty experience where expertise, cleanliness, attention to detail and genuine care create a feeling of trust and confidence.

Every customer is different. Every face has its own proportions. Every eye has its own expression. Every nail has its own needs. That is why we never follow a one-size-fits-all approach.

We listen first. We understand what you need. We assess. We recommend. We care.

You should always feel heard, comfortable, safe and personally looked after. A visit to MYNE should be a moment away from the rush of everyday life.

Beauty is in every detail. Trust is built through care.

In short

MYNE Nails & Brows is a personalised nail, brow and lash beauty studio located at Kallirrois 74, Koukaki, Athens, Greece. MYNE focuses on individualised nail care, professional brow mapping and personalised beauty treatments, with attention to cleanliness, detail and customer care.
